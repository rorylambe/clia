Our solution

Rest assured with Clia

One

Heart health assessment

Established clinical tests will facilitate a baseline assessment of your heart health. Rich, 24-hour health information from your wearable device will be incorporated into your Clia heart assessment as we learn about your body's baseline.

Two

See your heart's trends

Using the Clia app, you'll be able to see how your heart health evolves over time. You'll see improvements as you continue to exercise and follow your cardiac rehab plan.

Three

Community exercise

Through our app, find your exercise community with classes tailored specifically for people with cardiovascular disease.

Four

Connect with specialists

Using our clinician web-app, your healthcare provider can review your progress. You can also link with specialist Cardiac Rehabilitation Physiotherapists for expert consultations.
